I waited on the edge.
Unwilling to go inside my mind.
for once I entered
I'll be lost
sucked into the past
deeper and deeper.
into the whirlpool of memories
helpless against its pull.
The outside world beckons.
with the promise of a new life
But my mind still threatens
to hold me captive...
against the march of time
So I wait on the edge
unwilling to go inside
